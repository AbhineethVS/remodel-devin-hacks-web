How it works

Two ways to remodel. Pick by how far you want to go.

Reskin

Most websites

Keeps the page exactly where it is and repaints it with new colours, fonts, rounded or sharp corners, and shadows. One model call returns a small theme, and a deterministic engine applies it.

Time to apply
~10s
Layout shift, same fonts
0 px
Reuse on other sites
Instant
AI review passed

Rebuild

Wikipedia · HN · IRCTC

Designs a brand-new page from the content, with its own layout and scroll animations. The AI-written renderer runs in a sandbox with no extension access, then the AI reviews a screenshot of its own result.

Install

Running in a minute. Your key stays in your browser.

  1. 1
    Open chrome://extensions and turn on Developer mode.
  2. 2
    Click Load unpacked and choose the extension folder.
  3. 3
    Open any website and click Remodel in the bottom-right, or press Alt+Shift+M.
  4. 4
    Paste your OpenAI API key.
  5. 5
    Type a style and press Reskin this page, or Remodel this page on supported sites.

Good to know

  • Pictures and logos keep their original colours, so a logo drawn for a light background can look off on a dark skin.
  • On IRCTC, Remodel stops at train search. Login, booking and payment happen on the real IRCTC site.
  • New fonts are metric-matched, but a line can occasionally re-wrap.
